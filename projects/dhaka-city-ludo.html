Dhaka City 3D Ludo

A culturally immersive, browser-based 3D Ludo board game set on a Dhaka rooftop. Built entirely as a single-page web app with Three.js and Tone.js, it brings the nostalgic experience of playing Ludo during a rooftop "adda" to life, complete with rickshaw bells, CNG buzzes, street food vendors, and Kalboishakhi storms.

Features

More Than Just a Board Game

From procedural audio to first-person rooftop exploration, Dhaka City Ludo pushes the boundaries of what a browser game can be.

3D WebGL Board

Full Three.js-powered Ludo board with shadows, fog, and procedural geometry on an octagonal Dhaka rooftop.

Procedural Audio Engine

Real-time Tone.js soundscape with metro rail Doppler, diesel bus rumble, CNG putt-putt, rickshaw bells, and ambient BGM.

11 Weather Presets

Morning, Noon, Evening, Night, Full Moon, Rainy, Kalboishakhi Storm with lightning and flood waves, and more.

14+ Dhaka Twist Events

Traffic Jams, Load Shedding, Shantinagar Floods, WASA Digging, Mosquito Fogger, Rooftop Cat, Pigeon Frenzy, and more.

Online Multiplayer

Peer-to-peer via PeerJS/WebRTC DataChannels. No backend server needed. Share a room code via WhatsApp or Messenger.

First-Person Rooftop Walk

Full FPS mode with WASD movement, sprint, jump, and collision physics. Walk around and explore the rooftop.

12 Interactive NPCs

Tong Cha stall, Fuchka cart, Jhalmuri vendor, Paan kiosk, Kacchi Biriyani chef, BBQ grill, Guitar Adda, and more.

Toy Store and Projectiles

Buy and shoot traditional toys: Batul slingshot, bamboo bow, Paperfly planes, Latim spinning tops, and Fanush lanterns.

Bilingual UI and Mobile

Bengali and English throughout the interface. Touch-optimized with adaptive UI for phones and tablets.

Rooftop Explorer

Walk Around the Rooftop in First Person

Step away from the board and explore the rooftop in full first-person mode. Walk around with WASD controls, sprint, jump, and interact with the rich environment around you.

Discover 12 interactive NPC spots scattered across the rooftop - each with unique dialogue, sound effects, and mini-interactions that bring the Dhaka rooftop atmosphere to life.

Visit the Toy Store to buy traditional Bengali toys, then launch them as projectiles - slingshot clay guli, shoot bamboo arrows, fly Paperfly planes, spin Latim tops, throw Patka firecrackers, or release Fanush sky lanterns into the evening sky.

GitHub Pages

Run Locally

Clone and Play

WebGL support required. For best experience, use a Chromium-based browser with hardware acceleration enabled.

# Clone the repository
git clone git@github.com:nbslabs/dhaka-city-ludo.git
cd dhaka-city-ludo

# Serve with any HTTP server
python3 -m http.server 8000

# Or use Node.js
npx -y serve .

# Open http://localhost:8000
