Research and Engineering

Four Interconnected Research Tracks

Each track produces open-source tools, technical publications, or both. Every project begins as a research question and graduates into a tool when the answer proves useful.

Applied AI and Intelligent Systems

Building AI-powered tools and systems that solve real problems. AI agents, agentic workflows, retrieval-augmented generation systems, AI-native developer tools, and intelligent automation. We apply LLMs, embeddings, and ML pipelines where they create genuine leverage, not hype.

Algorithms and Data Structures

Original work on algorithms, data structures, and computational techniques. Novel approaches to graph problems, search optimizations, parsing, indexing, and code analysis. We publish implementations alongside explanations, making research accessible and reproducible.

Developer Infrastructure and Tooling

Production-grade open-source tools for developers. Configuration management, code analysis, documentation generation, knowledge graphs, and protocol-level tooling. These tools are not demos - they are things engineers install and rely on.

Core AI Research

Exploratory work in core AI areas: model behavior, prompt engineering science, agent architectures, evaluation methodologies, and novel applications of transformer-based systems. When we find something interesting, we write it up and release it.

dotai

Every AI coding assistant expects its config in a different file at a different path. dotai solves that - it unifies Claude Code, Gemini CLI, Antigravity, and GitHub Copilot under a single .ai/ directory, synced via symlinks. Write once, all tools stay in sync.

How We Work

A Lab That Ships Production Software

nbslabs is not structured like a traditional company or educational nonprofit. It operates like a research lab that happens to ship production software.

01

Research, Build, Release, Write

We identify a problem, research it deeply, build a tool that embodies our findings, release it as open source, and publish our learnings. The cycle then repeats.

02

Small Team, High Output

We intentionally stay small. A focused team working on carefully chosen problems produces better work than a large organization spreading itself thin.

03

Community Through Contribution

We build community by building things together, not by selling access to knowledge. Open issues, pull requests, and technical discussions are how relationships form.

04

Knowledge Sharing, Not Products

We occasionally run workshops and publish educational content, but these are byproducts of our research and building, not our core business.

Mission and Values

Why nbslabs Exists

To advance the state of AI tooling, algorithms, and applied intelligence through open-source engineering and original research, making our work freely available so that developers, researchers, and organizations worldwide can build on top of it.

Research First

Every project begins with a question worth answering, not a course worth selling. Our north star is original contribution to the field.

Open by Default

Every tool, dataset, model, and paper we produce is open-source. Knowledge compounds when it is shared.

Ship Real Artifacts

Research that lives only in notebooks is incomplete. We ship production-grade tools that people depend on.

Depth Over Breadth

We go deep on problems that matter. Rigorous understanding of fundamentals, algorithms, and systems is non-negotiable.

Global Impact, Local Roots

Proudly based in Bangladesh, aiming to demonstrate that world-class research and engineering can originate from anywhere.

Technology Philosophy

Principles That Guide Our Engineering

AI as Research Subject and Building Tool

We study AI systems, and we use AI systems to accelerate our own research and engineering. This dual relationship gives us practical insight that pure research labs and pure product teams often lack.

Algorithms Matter

In an era of "just prompt the model," we maintain that deep understanding of algorithms, data structures, and computational complexity is what separates engineers who build reliable systems from those who build fragile demos.

Interoperability as Design Principle

Our tools are built to work across ecosystems, not to create vendor lock-in. The AI tooling landscape is healthiest when tools compose rather than compete.

Reproducibility is Non-Negotiable

Every experiment, benchmark, and claim we publish comes with code, data, and instructions to reproduce it. If it cannot be reproduced, it is not research.
