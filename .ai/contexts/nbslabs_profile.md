# **1\.  Organization Overview**

nbslabs is a Bangladeshi developer community and open-source initiative dedicated to building the next generation of software engineers. Founded by working engineers who experienced first-hand the gap between university curricula and production-grade software development, nbslabs exists to close that gap — through hands-on coding, mentorship, open-source collaboration, and AI-augmented learning.

The organization operates under the belief that world-class software talent is not a function of geography or institution, but of environment, exposure, and community. Bangladesh has a rapidly growing pool of computer science graduates; nbslabs turns that potential into production-ready engineers.

# **2\.  Mission & Vision**

## **Mission**

To be a leading catalyst in Bangladesh for nurturing a new generation of skilled, innovative, and ethical software developers — fluent in modern tools, AI-powered workflows, and real-world problem-solving methodologies.

nbslabs empowers aspiring developers of all ages through open-source projects, accessible education, and community-driven workshops, with special focus on child coders and AI integration.

## **Vision**

A Bangladesh where any motivated learner — from a school-age child experimenting with Scratch to a university student building production APIs — has the community, the tools, and the mentorship to become a globally competitive software engineer.

# **4\.  Programs & Offerings**

nbslabs delivers impact through six interconnected programs, each designed to meet developers at their current skill level.

🤖  **AI-Powered Coding Workshops**

Members learn to integrate AI tooling — GitHub Copilot, Claude Code, prompt engineering, and agentic workflows — directly into their development practice. These sessions are not theoretical; participants build real features using real AI tools, developing the muscle memory to collaborate with AI effectively in professional settings.

🏫  **Community Workshops**

Free, live workshops run every 2–3 weeks, ranging from beginner-friendly sessions to advanced bootcamps. Topics span system design, backend engineering, API development, data structures, and DevOps practices. All sessions are recorded and made available publicly to maximize reach beyond Dhaka.

👦  **Kids Coding Club**

Weekend sessions for young learners using Scratch, Python, and JavaScript. The club is designed to spark curiosity and computational thinking before formal education channels those instincts into memorization. Alumni of the club are encouraged to return as junior mentors, creating a virtuous cycle.

💻  **Open-Source Project Contributions**

Members contribute to meaningful open-source projects — both nbslabs-owned tools (like dotai) and third-party ecosystems. Every contribution is a portfolio artifact. We provide structured contribution pathways so first-time contributors are never lost in a sea of GitHub issues.

🎓  **Premium Mentorship Program**

Selective, personalized guidance from industry practitioners. University students and junior developers are matched with mentors based on their career trajectory and technology interests. Mentors commit to structured check-ins, code reviews, and career coaching. The premium tier sustains the free community programs.

🎵  **Vibe Coding Sessions**

nbslabs's signature format: collaborative, flow-state coding sprints in a relaxed, creative atmosphere. Teams build projects together — often prototypes of tools they actually want to use — with AI tools as co-pilots. Think hackathon energy without the burnout.

# **5\.  Open-Source Projects**

Open-source software is the technical backbone of nbslabs. The organization maintains a growing portfolio of developer tools — primarily in the AI-native tooling space — published under the @nbslabs namespace on npm and GitHub.

## **dotai  (@nbslabs/dotai)**

The flagship project. dotai is a CLI tool that solves a painful problem in AI-augmented development: every AI coding assistant (Claude Code, GitHub Copilot, Gemini CLI, Cursor, Windsurf, etc.) expects its configuration in a different file at a different path. dotai unifies all those configurations under a single .ai/ directory and manages them via symlinks — so developers write once and all tools stay in sync.

# **7\.  Technology Philosophy**

nbslabs does not teach technology in the abstract — it teaches engineers how to ship. The curriculum and tooling are anchored to technologies that matter in production: Java/Spring, Node.js, React, Python, SQL databases, and the emerging layer of AI-native tooling.

AI is not a replacement for engineering skill at nbslabs — it is an amplifier of it. Members learn to use tools like GitHub Copilot, Claude Code, Gemini CLI, and Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers as force multipliers, not as shortcuts that erode understanding. The goal is engineers who can build and critically evaluate AI-generated code.

Open standards and interoperability are core design principles. dotai, for example, refuses to create lock-in: it syncs configurations across all major AI coding tools simultaneously, ensuring developers are never dependent on a single vendor's ecosystem.

The organization publishes technical writing covering AI tools, developer tooling, system design, and emerging research. # **9\.  Strategic Roadmap**

nbslabs is at an early but intentional stage of growth. The roadmap is organized into three horizons:

### **Horizon 1 — Foundation (2025)**

* Establish regular workshop cadence; reach 100+ active community members.

* Publish dotai v2.0.0 with the knowledge layer and MCP stdio server.

* Launch structured Kids Coding Club curriculum.

* Open mentorship program to first cohort of university students.

### **Horizon 2 — Scale (2026)**

* Expand workshop reach to major university campuses outside Dhaka.

* Launch WikiForge and codeograph as production-ready open-source tools.

* Publish @nbslabs packages beyond dotai; build collaborative maintainer team.

* Introduce advanced AI engineering bootcamp with certification pathway.

### **Horizon 3 — Ecosystem (2027+)**

* Position nbslabs as the reference developer community for AI-native engineering in Bangladesh.

* Establish partnerships with universities and tech companies for structured internship pipelines.

* Explore grant and sponsorship models to sustain free community programs indefinitely.

* Export the community model to other emerging-market developer communities in South and Southeast Asia.
